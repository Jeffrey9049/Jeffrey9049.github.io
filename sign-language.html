Research area 02 / AI + Linguistics

Making sign language computationally accessible.

My Taiwan Sign Language (TSL / 臺灣手語) work investigates how body movement, hand articulation, and linguistic annotations can support machine learning for sign recognition and language analysis.

Multimodal learningMediaPipePyTorchELAN annotations臺灣手語
Core problem

From motion to meaning.

Sign recognition requires more than identifying a single hand pose: movement, orientation, location, timing, and linguistic context all matter.

Motion representations

Extract spatiotemporal body and hand landmarks from recorded signing, and construct aligned sequences for modeling.

Linguistic information

Connect visual signals with ELAN annotations such as gloss, handshape, palm orientation, location, and movement.

Generalizable recognition

Study performance across different signers, visually similar vocabulary items, and varied recording conditions.

Research workflow

A multimodal pipeline.

A modular workflow connects data acquisition, representation learning, linguistic alignment, and evaluation.

STEP 01

Capture

Collect signing videos and derive pose/hand landmarks.

STEP 02

Align

Synchronize landmark sequences with ELAN gloss and linguistic tiers.

STEP 03

Model

Train sequence models and compare feature representations.

STEP 04

Evaluate

Measure recognition accuracy and robustness across conditions.
