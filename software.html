Why I Develop These Tools

Technical barriers often make AI difficult to use in sign language research. People who understand sign language and linguistic annotation may not be familiar with programming, while technology developers may not have the same domain knowledge. I develop these tools to help connect those communities and support more independent experimentation by sign language experts.

My goal is to lower both the technical and hardware barriers to participation: no programming is needed for the intended workflows, and neither application is designed to require a dedicated graphics card. Windows and macOS editions are available in both interface languages. Actual performance depends on the computer and the size of the video or dataset.
