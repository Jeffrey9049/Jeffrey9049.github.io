Lan-Jun Jeffrey Chang

Communications Engineering · Network Optimization · Machine Learning

I am a researcher with a background in communications engineering, network optimization, and machine learning. I received my M.S. in Communications Engineering from National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan. My previous research focused on robust routing and network capacity planning under uncertainty, with findings published in IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management.

I am currently a research assistant at the Graduate Institute of Linguistics, National Chung Cheng University, where I investigate linguistically informed multimodal learning for Taiwan Sign Language. My current research interests include robust motion capture, spatial semantics, and discourse-level language modeling, with the long-term goal of developing sign language translation and generation systems. I also develop accessible AI tools to lower the technical and computational barriers to sign language research.
