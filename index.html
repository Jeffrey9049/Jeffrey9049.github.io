Academic profile

Lan-jun Jeffrey Chang

Communications Engineering · Network Optimization · Multimodal Machine Learning

I am a researcher in communications engineering with interests in network optimization and multimodal machine learning. I received my M.S. in Communications Engineering from National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan. My research examines robust routing under uncertain traffic demands and network capacities, as well as computational approaches to Taiwan Sign Language.

I currently work as a research assistant at the Graduate Institute of Linguistics, National Chung Cheng University, contributing to Taiwan Sign Language corpus processing and machine-learning research.
