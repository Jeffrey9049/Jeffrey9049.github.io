Lan-Jun Jeffrey Chang

Communications Engineering · Network Optimization · Machine Learning

I am a researcher with a background in communications engineering, network optimization, and machine learning. I received my M.S. in Communications Engineering from National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan. My previous work focuses on robust routing and network capacity planning under uncertainty, with results published in IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management.

Currently, I am a research assistant at the Graduate Institute of Linguistics, National Chung Cheng University, where I work on Taiwan Sign Language corpus analysis and multimodal machine-learning methods. My broader interests include AI for communication networks, software-defined networking, and semantic and task-oriented communications.
