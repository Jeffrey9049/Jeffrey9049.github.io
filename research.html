Research

My primary research interests are in network optimization, particularly robust routing, capacity planning, and resource allocation in communication networks.

Network Optimization

Communication networks operate under variations in traffic, available capacity, and system conditions. My work investigates optimization methods that make routing and capacity planning more resilient to uncertainty while remaining computationally tractable.

Oblivious Routing Under Demand and Capacity Uncertainty

This work studies static routing decisions when both traffic demand and link capacity are uncertain. We formulate a robust optimization problem and derive a linear programming approach that enables solutions to be computed efficiently. The goal is to reduce worst-case performance degradation relative to traffic-aware routing.

Robust optimization · Oblivious routing · Linear programming · Uncertain link capacity

Capacity Planning for Hose-Model VPN Networks

This research addresses network capacity planning under the hose traffic model, in which each endpoint has bounded aggregate incoming and outgoing traffic rather than a single fixed traffic matrix. The approach considers routing and capacity allocation across admissible traffic demands.

Fog and Software-Defined Networking

In an earlier research project, I investigated fog node placement for data-intensive Internet of Things environments, including optimization-based and approximation approaches to the placement problem.

Additional Interests

My broader interests include distributed intelligence, AI-assisted networking, wireless resource allocation, and semantic and task-oriented communications. These are areas I am exploring in relation to my foundations in network optimization.
