PROJECT 01 / MASTER'S THESIS & JOURNAL PAPER

Oblivious Routing for Networks With Dual Uncertainties in Demand and Capacity

Conventional routing decisions become challenging when both traffic matrices and link capacities are unpredictable. This work studies static routing that limits performance loss relative to demand-aware, capacity-aware routing decisions.

  • Joint uncertainty in traffic demand and link capacity
  • Robust optimization and linear-programming-based formulation
  • Evaluation of routing performance across network scenarios
